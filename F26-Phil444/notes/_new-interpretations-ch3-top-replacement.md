Now it's a somewhat delicate philosophical question just what this assumption amounts to. While the assumption is more or less universally made by game theorists, there is less agreement about just what it comes to. There are three natural interpretations, which we might call the **objective chance**, **epistemic** and **frequentist** readings. We'll leave them there for now; the question of which is right doesn't bite until we can see what a mixed equilibrium asks of a player, so we'll come back to it at the end of the chapter.
