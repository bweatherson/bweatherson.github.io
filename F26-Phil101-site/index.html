Readings

There is no textbook. Everything on the schedule is linked below, and all of it is free except one item: the Datta paper for Lecture 7 is on JSTOR, which resolves from a campus connection or through the library proxy, and which allows a limited number of free article views from anywhere else.

The two Mill readings are extracts typeset for this course, and are under Course Materials above rather than in the table.

Where a reading names a section, read that section only. The encyclopedia entries in particular are a good deal longer than the part we want.

Instructions for Slides

The slides are built using revealjs. To move around them, the four key things to know are:

  1. Left and right arrows move between sections.
  2. Up and down arrows move between slides within a section.
  3. If you move the cursor to the top left corner a three-bar menu appears. Click on it and you can move between any slides.
  4. Or simply use space bar to advance one slide forward at any time.
